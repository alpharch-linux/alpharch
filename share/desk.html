A desk you can make yours.

Move & resize. Drag a chart title; drag any edge to resize. Double-click the title, or use its expand button, to focus a chart. Use Arrange for a clean tiled layout.

Work inside a chart. Wheel over the plot to zoom time at the cursor; wheel over the price scale to zoom price. Drag the plot to inspect history; Shift-drag moves price. Drag the time or price axis to stretch it. Fit resets both scales, Auto restores price fitting, and Live returns to the latest bar. Focus the plot and use +/−, arrows, Home, A or 0. Hover a candle for exact OHLC and volume. Open ƒ for searchable indicators, then edit, hide, or remove each study.

Make it yours. Choose a palette, rename your desk, and save a named layout. Saved layouts stay in this browser. Export a copy from the Desk menu.

Keyboard. Tab moves between controls; Enter activates. On a focused chart title, Arrow keys move the tile and Shift + Arrow resize it. Escape closes a dialog or exits chart focus.

Live data preview. Charts use the named public exchange. Candles can include historical OHLCV; delta, footprints, profiles and depth use captured events only. Market trades can have gaps after interruptions, which are marked. This browser workspace does not execute trades. The trade-live launcher opens independent native app windows; Review opens local replay and optional session tools. IBKR futures require the optional official SDK and your signed-in local gateway. CQG/Rithmic adapters remain access-dependent development work.

Price alerts

Local crossing alerts while this desk is open, evaluated from received updates (about once per second). A crossing between updates can be missed. One-shot; rearm after triggering. Reconnection establishes a new baseline and does not invent a crossing during the gap.
