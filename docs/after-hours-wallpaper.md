## Generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: a finished premium desktop wallpaper for Alpharch, a futures and crypto trading environment on Omarchy Linux. Landscape 16:9, ideally 3840x2160, high resolution.
Input image: exact existing Alpharch logo, used ONLY as the brand-shape reference. Preserve its recognizable geometry: short angular waveform joins a circular alpha body with a descending curved right leg. Do not invent a different logo.
Create a bold, art-directed wallpaper called AFTER HOURS. Design the reference emblem as a real, precisely machined dimensional object, champagne titanium and warm copper edges, in a low oblique frontal view. Give it beautiful weight, brushed surface detail, crisp bevels and a slightly worn tactile finish. Place it centrally, at moderate scale, against layered dark petrol architectural planes. Include an elegant asymmetrical sweep of fine parallel copper fins behind the emblem, with restrained electric teal and a small oxblood/vermilion reflection at their edges, like high-end instrument manufacturing. The fins should feel structured and directional, reminiscent of stacked liquidity, not a graph. Strong directional studio light, deep shadows, selective specular highlights. Rich color that is intentional, with plenty of quiet deep petrol/ink area at outer edges for tiled desktop windows. The artwork should feel collectible, a confident motorsport or record-sleeve art direction, technical craftsmanship, energetic and sophisticated. Not an app landing-page graphic.
At bottom center, leave generous space around small exquisitely typeset uppercase wordmark text exactly "ALPHARCH", with a much smaller supporting line exactly "LINUX IS FOR TRADERS". Keep all logo and typography in the central safe area for desktop crops. No other words. No literal chart, prices, candlesticks, UI panels, computer/monitor frame, stock market arrows, coins, money, bull/bear mascots, city skyline, nebula, glowing circuit board, decorative HUD, random particles, generic rainbow fluid blobs, or watermarks. The result fills the entire image; it is the wallpaper itself, not a photograph of a monitor.
